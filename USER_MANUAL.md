# OSPREY User Manual

## 1. Getting Started
*   **Sign Up:** Enter your email, password, and Company Name. The system creates your dedicated tenant.
*   **Dashboard:** Your central command center showing Active Nodes, Team Members, and System Status.

## 2. Organization Feed
*   **Timeline:** View updates from all departments.
*   **Context:** Click on a post attached to a document (e.g., "PO #123") to view the source document.
*   **Comments:** Discuss approvals or issues directly in the feed.

## 3. Core Operations
### Finance
*   **Journal Entries:** Post double-entry records. The system auto-validates balance.
*   **Reports:** Access Income Statements and Balance Sheets in the Reporting module.

### Inventory
*   **Stores:** Manage multiple locations (Warehouses, Sites).
*   **Movements:** Receive or Issue stock.
*   **Blind Receiving:** Warehouse staff verify goods without seeing the PO Quantity first.

### HR & Payroll
*   **Employees:** Manage contracts and biodata.
*   **Payroll:** Run monthly payrolls. The "Formula Builder" handles complex taxes (PAYE, NSSF).

## 4. Gate Pass System
1.  **Request:** Employee requests a pass for items/visitors.
2.  **Approval:** Manager approves via the App.
3.  **QR Code:** A unique QR is generated.
4.  **Scan:** Guard scans the QR at the gate to log entry/exit.

# Role Manuals

## Guard
*   **Login:** Use your specific credentials.
*   **Action:** Tap "Scan QR". Point camera at visitor's pass.
*   **Verify:** Confirm name and items matches screen.

## HR Manager
*   **Onboarding:** Use "Bulk Import" to upload staff Excel.
*   **Payroll:** Go to Payroll > Run. Click "Calculate" to execute the formula engine.

## Procurement Officer
*   **LPO:** Convert approved PRs to LPOs.
*   **Budget:** System will block you if the department budget is exhausted.
