# PWA Installation Guide

OSPREY is a Progressive Web App (PWA). It works offline and can be installed on your device.

## Desktop (Chrome/Edge)
1.  Navigate to the OSPREY URL.
2.  Look for the "Install" icon in the address bar (Monitor icon with down arrow).
3.  Click "Install OSPREY".
4.  The app will launch in its own window.

## Mobile (iOS)
1.  Open OSPREY in Safari.
2.  Tap the "Share" button (Square with up arrow).
3.  Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
4.  Confirm name and tap "Add".

## Mobile (Android)
1.  Open OSPREY in Chrome.
2.  Tap the three dots menu.
3.  Tap "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen".
4.  Follow prompts.
